Serre AI is an independent AI research lab in NYC. We study reasoning in AI systems
through computational complexity, verification and large-scale empirical evaluation of
language models.

### Open source

| Project | What it does | Status | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Fib](https://github.com/serre-ai/serre-fib) | A small local model that flags Python docstrings that no longer match their functions. | Pre-alpha | Apache-2.0 |

Each project is planned in the open: its backlog, decisions and roadmap live in its
repository.
